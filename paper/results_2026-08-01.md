system. Its macro precision/recall/F1 are 0.525/0.520/0.460, compared with
0.594/0.473/0.448 for the baseline. The result is consistent with the intended
trade-off: more recall and slightly better F1 at the cost of precision.

## Empty-answer behavior

Treating an empty object list as the positive class, the baseline obtains
precision 0.5510, recall 0.8710, and F1 0.6750. The relaxed configuration
obtains precision 0.6957, recall 0.6882, and F1 0.6919. Thus the relaxed system
improves empty-answer F1 by 0.0169, although it predicts fewer empty rows.

## Interpretation and next controlled experiments

The largest remaining gaps are numeric relations and death city. Awards also
remain weak, but the relaxed prompt produced a meaningful improvement. The
next experiments should therefore compare a model-selected numeric candidate
on only `hasArea` and `hasCapacity`, iterative award completion on only the ten
award rows, and the relation-specific soft gate on exchange and death-city
rows. Relation outputs should be merged into the full system only after an
honest, relation-level improvement. The border workflow should remain fixed.
